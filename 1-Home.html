COSMIC KNOWLEDGE

DID YOU KNOW?

Curious bits of information from across our solar system and beyond.

FACT #01

One million Earths could fit inside the Sun – and the Sun is considered an average-size star.

FACT #02

Scientists revealed evidence of intermittent running water on Mars, challenging the belief that Earth was unique in liquid water.

FACT #03

Comets are leftovers from our solar system creation 4.5 billion years ago, made of sand, ice, and carbon dioxide.

FACT #04

You wouldn’t be able to walk on Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, or Neptune because they have no solid surface!

FACT #05

If you could fly a commercial airplane to Pluto, the entire trip would take more than 800 years!

FACT #06

There are over 500,000 pieces of space junk orbiting Earth, including fragments and tools lost during ISS construction.

FACT #07

A car-sized asteroid enters Earth’s atmosphere roughly once a year, but burns up safely before reaching us.

FACT #08

The highest mountain known is on asteroid Vesta, measuring 22km high — three times the height of Mount Everest!

FACT #09

There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on all beaches on Earth — at least a billion trillion!

FACT #10

The sunset on Mars appears distinctly blue due to dust particles scattering blue light in its atmosphere.
