HOW THE SOLAR SYSTEM FORMED

From a cloud of gas and dust to the Solar System we know today.

01
☁️

Cosmic Cloud

A huge cloud of gas and dust existed in space. Gravity slowly pulled the material together.

02
🌀

Collapse & Rotation

The cloud collapsed under gravity and began spinning faster, forming a rotating disk.

03
☀️

The Sun Is Born

Most of the material moved toward the center, creating the young Sun.

04
✨

Planets Form

Dust and rocks collided and joined together, eventually forming planets and smaller bodies.

05
🌌

A Solar System Emerges

Over millions of years, the planets settled into stable orbits around the Sun.
